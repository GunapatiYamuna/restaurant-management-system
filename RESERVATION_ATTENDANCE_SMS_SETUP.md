# Free Reservation Attendance Notifications

The reservation attendance confirmation is now completely free and works inside the FoodieHub website.

## How it works

1. A customer books a table.
2. The reservation stays **Pending** with attendance response **Pending**.
3. When the customer is logged in and the reservation is within one hour of its start time, FoodieHub shows an attendance notification in the customer's reservation page.
4. The customer chooses:
   - **Coming** → reservation becomes **Confirmed**
   - **Not Coming** → reservation becomes **Cancelled**
5. The restaurant portal displays the customer's attendance response.

## No paid SMS service is required

You do **not** need:

- Twilio Account SID
- Twilio Auth Token
- Twilio phone number
- PUBLIC_BASE_URL
- SMS credits

The browser checks the free notification endpoint while the customer is using FoodieHub. The notification is generated from the existing Django reservation data.

## Testing

For a college-project demo:

1. Log in as a customer.
2. Create a reservation for a time within the next hour.
3. Open **My Reservations**.
4. The attendance notification will appear automatically.
5. Click **Coming** or **Not Coming**.
6. Refresh the restaurant reservation portal and verify the attendance response and reservation status.

The customer can also use the attendance links generated by the website notification. The signed links expire after 48 hours and cannot be used for another customer's reservation.
